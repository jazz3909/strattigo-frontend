Question 3

Which enzyme catalyzes the first committed step of glycolysis and is the pathway's main regulatory point?

A Hexokinase
B Phosphofructokinase-1 ✓
C Pyruvate kinase
D Aldolase
✓ Correct

Phosphofructokinase-1 (PFK-1) catalyzes the first committed step — once fructose-6-phosphate is phosphorylated, the molecule is locked into glycolysis. Hexokinase acts earlier, but its product can still be diverted elsewhere, so it isn't the committed step.
